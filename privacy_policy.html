Privacy Policy for ContactAI

ContactAI ("we", "our", or "us") provides a contacts and call-management application that helps users manage contacts, view call history, and receive call-related notifications. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

a. Contact Information (Read/Write Contacts)

To allow you to view, add, edit, and manage contacts, our app accesses your device’s Contacts database.

✔ We do not upload or store your contacts on any server.
✔ All contact data stays only on your device.

b. Call Logs (Read Call History)

We access call logs only to display:

  • Your recent calls
  • Missed calls
  • Dialed and received calls
This data is processed locally and never shared externally.

c. Phone State

Phone state information is used to detect:

  • Incoming calls
  • Outgoing calls
  • Missed calls
  • Call end events (cut/disconnected)
This is required to trigger notifications and update call history.

d. Device Information for Ads

We display in-app advertisements using third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob. These partners may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Approximate location (for ad relevance)
  • App usage data related to ads
You can opt out of personalized ads via device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Show call history and call logs
  • Manage contacts, favorites, and editing options
  • Send missed-call and call-disconnect notifications
  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Serve advertisements and measure ad effectiveness

We do not sell or share your personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

Advertising (AdMob)

Our app uses Google AdMob for displaying ads. AdMob may collect device information as described above.
Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Crash Reporting / Analytics (If used)

If we use Firebase or similar tools, they may collect non-personal analytics and crash information.

4. Data Storage & Security

✔ We do not store any call logs or contacts on our servers.
✔ All sensitive information remains on your device.
✔ We use reasonable security measures to protect your data.

5. Permissions Used

  • READ_CONTACTS / WRITE_CONTACTS – To manage contacts
  • READ_CALL_LOG – To show call history
  • READ_PHONE_STATE – For call detection
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – For missed-call alerts
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – Required for ads

6. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not target children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Choices

  • You may disable personalized ads in device settings.
  • You may deny permissions (but some features may not work).
  • You may uninstall the app at any time to stop data processing.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page.
